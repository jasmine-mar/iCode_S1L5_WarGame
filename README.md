A program that is supposed to simulate playing the game War against the computer.

Explanation of War:
War is a simple card game, played using a standard playing card deck, usually with two players. 
The deck of cards is split evenly among the players, facedown. 
Every turn, each player will grab the card at the top of their deck, and place it faceup in front of them.
The player that has the card with the highest value takes all the cards played that round and adds it to their stack.
In the event that the players have cards of equal value, the players must "go to war".
Each player with place 3 cards facedown, and 1 card faceup. The person that has the faceup card with the higher value will take all the cards that have been placed.
The game goes on until one player has won all of the cards in the deck.
